## 🎯 Business Problem

Tech Savvy Solutions had accumulated marketing campaign data that was inconsistent, incomplete, and difficult to analyze.

The business needed a structured analytics solution to:

- Evaluate overall marketing campaign performance
- Identify high- and low-performing campaigns
- Understand revenue performance across marketing channels
- Analyse changes in revenue over the quarter
- Assess the relationship between advertising spend and leads
- Forecast potential Q4 revenue
- Support better marketing budget allocation and strategic planning
---

## ❓ Business Questions

This analysis was designed to answer the following business questions:

- Which marketing campaigns delivered the highest ROI?
- Which marketing channels generated the most revenue?
- How did revenue change across July, August, and September?
- Why did some campaigns outperform others?
- What relationship exists between advertising spend and leads generated?
- What revenue could be expected in Q4 based on Q3 performance?
- What strategic actions could improve future campaign performance?

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. July Generated the Highest Revenue

July generated **₦34.44 million**, making it the strongest revenue month of Q3.

Revenue declined to **₦18.04 million in August** and **₦16.29 million in September**, indicating a downward trend after July.

### 2. Advertising Spend Did Not Guarantee More Leads

The analysis found a **weak positive correlation (r = 0.448)** between advertising spend and leads generated.

This suggests that increasing advertising spend alone did not necessarily guarantee stronger lead generation.

### 3. Referral Programme Delivered the Highest ROI

The **Referral Programme** recorded the highest campaign ROI at **3,100%**.

This indicates that referral-based marketing delivered particularly strong returns relative to its recorded advertising investment.

### 4. Paid Ads Generated the Highest Revenue

**Paid Ads generated ₦17.63 million**, making it the highest-revenue marketing channel in the analysis.

### 5. Campaign Performance Varied Significantly

The campaign analysis showed substantial differences in performance across campaigns, highlighting the importance of evaluating individual campaign results rather than relying only on overall marketing performance.

### 6. Q4 Revenue Was Forecast to Decline

Based on the Q3 trend analysis, Q4 revenue was projected to decline progressively:

- **October:** ₦11.62M
- **November:** ₦8.29M
- **December:** ₦5.91M

The forecast was based on the average Q3 month-over-month growth rate of **-28.66%**.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were developed:

### 1. Increase Investment in High-Performing Campaigns

Allocate more resources to campaigns that consistently demonstrate strong ROI and revenue performance, particularly campaigns such as the **Referral Programme**.

### 2. Review and Optimise Underperforming Campaigns

Campaigns with weak or limited returns should be reviewed to identify opportunities to improve targeting, messaging, channel selection, and overall campaign effectiveness.

### 3. Look Beyond Advertising Spend

Since the relationship between advertising spend and leads was relatively weak, marketing decisions should consider **campaign quality, audience targeting, and channel performance**, rather than relying on increased spend alone.

### 4. Monitor Performance Regularly

Key marketing KPIs should be monitored regularly to identify performance changes early and support timely business decisions.

### 5. Automate Marketing Performance Reporting

The reporting process could be automated using **Power BI scheduled refreshes**, reducing manual reporting effort and making campaign performance information easier to monitor.
